# Daily AI news

> Related: [shared scheduling contract](scheduled-tasks.md) · [ADR-0016](../adr/0016-daily-news-owner-handoff.md) · [ADR-0017](../adr/0017-daily-news-results-and-archives.md)
>
> Resilience implementation: local candidate on 2026-10-16. The user approved nonblocking archive failure and then continued after the seven-state message prototype. Deployment and real-Weixin acceptance are separate.

## Problem Statement

Generated daily news can remain unsent after bot credentials expire. Recovery must include older cached reports. The installed application can move its bundled Codex executable while the service remains running. Mechanically truncating each part of a long summary also loses the article's central argument and necessary qualifications. A source failure can disappear as an empty result, and an archive-stage refetch can discard an article that already has a successful summary. Reprocessing the same successful article wastes work and creates inconsistent summaries.

## Solution

Retain generated reports and resume eligible delivery for the original enabled account and recipient after credentials and conversation context recover. Deliver cached reports in date order without repeating uncertain or accepted submissions. Generate complete, source-grounded short summaries within the message budget, using natural prose rather than four mandatory sections. Preserve successful results per article and reuse them on later encounters. If HN remains unavailable after bounded retries, deliver the other completed content with an explicit missing-source explanation. Saved source counts select general AI news, the current HN hot list, or both within ten articles. HN has no built-in AI/LLM-only filter. Discovering a new ranking does not authorise rewriting a successful article; it may redisplay a saved result in a new authorised occurrence.

## Functional Requirements

### daily-ai-news::REQ-001 Cached recovery

- daily-ai-news::REQ-001/AC-01: After the original bot is rebound and the original recipient establishes a fresh conversation, automatically submit reports waiting for login or context recovery, using their persisted message and digest without repeating research or archive work.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-001/AC-02: Persist queued content across service restarts, including reports saved before submission starts.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-001/AC-03: Reconcile cached delivery against the persisted conversation refresh on service startup, channel-context changes and scheduled work. A newer usable context must resume the original recipient’s queue without requiring another message merely because the refresh event preceded startup.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-001/AC-04: Persist the conversation refresh used for each delivery attempt. A rejected stale context must not be retried automatically until a strictly newer refresh exists; legacy records use their last failure time as the conservative comparison boundary.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-001/AC-05: Existing status and local failure notifications identify the retained report count, oldest report date and current required action. Once context is recovered, do not continue instructing the user to refresh solely because an old report recorded a stale context.

### daily-ai-news::REQ-002 Chronological delivery

- daily-ai-news::REQ-002/AC-01: Select eligible reports by ascending report date and submit them serially. Changes to record update order must not affect delivery order.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-002/AC-02: A newly generated automatic report must wait behind older recoverable cached reports for the same recipient.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-002/AC-03: If a submission fails, stop that drain. A later fresh inbound may continue with still-eligible reports, but must not retry terminal records.

### daily-ai-news::REQ-003 Executable recovery

- daily-ai-news::REQ-003/AC-01: Resolve the installed bundled Codex executable at each new news-process invocation, supporting both observed application layouts and a stale explicit path to the former bundled layout.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-003/AC-02: Preserve a valid explicit custom executable and fail when an explicit custom executable is unavailable; do not silently substitute a different runtime.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-003/AC-03: Service startup and LaunchAgent installation use the same resolution rule.

### daily-ai-news::REQ-004 Complete summaries

- daily-ai-news::REQ-004/AC-01: Newly generated summaries express the article's central argument in natural prose. Neither the detailed archive summary nor the short delivery summary requires background/development/evidence/impact fields or labels. Preserve qualifications needed to avoid changing the argument; do not invent facts or merely paraphrase the title.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-004/AC-02: Short delivery summaries are complete sentences, not character-cut fragments or ellipsis substitutions. A new short summary has at most 80 Unicode code points, punctuation included. Retain the existing 280-code-point item and 1,500-code-point message limits for up to ten articles. Metadata is included in those budgets.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-004/AC-03: An invalid short summary receives one bounded rewrite attempt. If it remains invalid, use the existing research failure/selection behavior; never silently truncate it for delivery. Other process or structural failures do not trigger this content rewrite.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-004/AC-04: Persist the short summary separately from the full archive summary, and reuse it in preview, sending and cached recovery. Existing frozen outbound messages retain their exact body; legacy previews without a short summary may show a complete fitting original summary, otherwise explicitly indicate that the legacy short summary is unavailable and point to the local detailed archive, rather than fabricate or cut a viewpoint.

### daily-ai-news::REQ-005 Reuse successfully processed articles

Applies to: repeated encounters with the same article in this specialised daily-news task. Reusing a summary and deciding whether an article belongs in today's report are separate decisions.

- daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-01: An article is successfully processed only when its validated detailed and short summaries are durably available together with its stable identity and source reference. An in-memory response or a success flag without a usable saved result cannot establish success.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-02: Encountering that successful article again in the same batch, on a later day, on a new HN ranking or in an ordinary run-now reuses its saved result or skips it under applicable eligibility rules. It does not fetch the article body or generate a summary again. Legacy eligibility validation follows REQ-009/AC-05–06 without rewriting the summary. Candidate discovery and ranking metadata may refresh independently.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-03: A subsequent archive or delivery failure does not reset successful article processing. Continuing an authorised attempt with A/B/C successful and D failed processes only the unfinished work; it preserves the saved results for A/B/C.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-04: Concurrent candidates for the same article cannot independently generate competing summaries. An uncertain interrupted operation is reconciled under [scheduled-tasks::REQ-005/AC-03](scheduled-tasks.md), not treated as a safe automatic retry.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-05: Cleanup must not turn a previously successful article into an unseen article. Missing or corrupt retained output is reported as unavailable/inconsistent; it is neither silently treated as a usable summary nor automatically regenerated. Current-HN selection follows daily-ai-news::REQ-008/AC-02; other delivery deduplication and frozen-message rules remain independent of article processing.

### daily-ai-news::REQ-006 Explicit HN degradation

Applies to: an execution where HN remains unavailable after its bounded attempts and other valid news content has completed. Archive failure follows daily-ai-news::REQ-007 independently of source completeness.

- daily-ai-news::REQ-006/AC-01: Preserve the other completed content and use it for the report instead of failing or discarding the whole research result solely because HN is unavailable.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-006/AC-02: The delivered report explicitly names HN as missing for this execution and gives a concise, evidence-based reason. A source exception is not presented as a successful empty result, and degraded content is not described as a complete report.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-006/AC-03: Content completeness is distinct from channel acceptance. Delivering every part of a degraded report can be channel-accepted; this does not mean all sources succeeded. A channel-partial or unknown outcome still obeys daily-ai-news::CON-001/AC-01.

### daily-ai-news::REQ-007 Archive failure does not block delivery

- daily-ai-news::REQ-007/AC-01: Once valid article results and the complete outgoing message are durably saved, failure to write the user-readable archive does not withhold that report from normal eligible delivery. An archive failure does not remove a successfully processed article from the report.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-007/AC-02: Retain the failed archive work and retry it independently using the saved bodies and summaries under the common owner. Archive retry never refetches an article, regenerates a summary, changes the frozen report or submits another message; retry exhaustion remains visible rather than being called archive success.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-007/AC-03: Inspection distinguishes article/content, archive and delivery outcomes. A failed mandatory result-store or outgoing-message persistence is still a hard stop; nonblocking user-readable archive failure does not authorise sending unsaved content.

### daily-ai-news::REQ-008 Execute the current source selection

- daily-ai-news::REQ-008/AC-01: Resolve the frozen research instruction to validated source counts before discovery; a zero-count source is not researched. Counts are configurable within the existing total budget of ten articles. Invalid or unsupported selection fails explicitly rather than silently using a different count or source. This does not mutate task settings or interpret a new management request.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-008/AC-02: HN Top N means the first N eligible articles in the current hot-list order, not N previously undelivered articles. Apply the saved past-24-hour window to HN submission metadata before selection. Previously delivered successful articles may appear in a new independently authorised occurrence using their unchanged saved summaries; the same occurrence is still never replayed.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-008/AC-03: If eligible verified articles within the bounded candidate scan cannot satisfy the requested count, deliver the completed content with requested/actual counts and the observed shortfall reason. Failed reads, unavailable known results and time-window exclusions must not become a silent complete result. No fabricated replacement, summary truncation or automatic supplemental push is permitted.

### daily-ai-news::REQ-009 Candidate/body correspondence

Applies to: newly processed HN articles, locally fetched general-news articles and legacy successful results encountered under the common scheduled owner. This is an admission check, not a guarantee that a model can establish every factual claim.

- daily-ai-news::REQ-009/AC-01: Before recording article success, assess whether the supplied body supports the subject, event or central question of the candidate title. Equivalent wording and translations are allowed. Require an explicit matching assessment and a supporting body excerpt; a missing, insufficient, mismatched or structurally invalid assessment cannot establish success. Metadata alone and invented excerpts are not body evidence.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-009/AC-02: Rejected articles do not enter the successful ranking, report or archive. Continue through later eligible candidates within the existing scan/deadline limits; disclose an observed mismatch or unverifiable-content reason when the final selection is short, following daily-ai-news::REQ-008/AC-03. Do not turn an advertisement or a statement that the article is unavailable into a replacement news item.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-009/AC-03: A content rejection does not trigger the short-summary rewrite. If an otherwise valid summary needs its permitted brief rewrite, recheck the replacement assessment before admission. Combine new-article assessment with the existing summary call; do not add a second routine model pass.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-009/AC-04: Retain a rejected body as failed evidence until a later eligible processing attempt, but do not reuse that rejected body for the later attempt. A successful result continues to obey daily-ai-news::REQ-005; this change does not rewrite frozen historical messages or silently regenerate successful summaries.

- daily-ai-news::REQ-009/AC-05: On the first later encounter with a historical successful result that predates content assessment, validate the saved body and existing summary once against the candidate. Preserve the original body, detailed summary and brief unchanged; matching results remain reusable, while mismatched or insufficient results are excluded from new reports. Do not refetch or generate a replacement summary.
- daily-ai-news::REQ-009/AC-06: Persist the legacy-validation claim before the external call and retain its outcome across restarts. Concurrent encounters cannot duplicate validation; an interrupted or failed validation stays unavailable without automatic repeat. A fenced or cancelled worker cannot publish a late matching verdict. Historical audit and already frozen reports remain readable and unchanged.

## Constraints

### daily-ai-news::CON-001 Submission safety

- daily-ai-news::CON-001/AC-01: Never automatically resubmit reports whose result is unknown, partially accepted, accepted, or a non-recoverable rejection. An interrupted sending record becomes unknown.
- daily-ai-news::CON-001/AC-02: Recheck the enabled account, recipient, and usable credentials before every submission. Disabling news or changing the account/recipient stops queued delivery; rebinding a different bot does not authorize forwarding old content.
- daily-ai-news::CON-001/AC-03: Concurrent inbound, research, and recovery operations must not submit the same report twice or let a later report overtake an older pending report.

## Failure modes and boundaries

- Login confirmation clears conversation tokens. Confirmation alone cannot send a report; a fresh inbound from the same account and recipient supplies the missing context (daily-ai-news::REQ-001/AC-01).
- Context or bot credentials fail again during recovery: retain content, stop the current drain, and wait for a later recovery event (daily-ai-news::REQ-002/AC-03).
- A submission times out, throws, is partially accepted, or is interrupted: preserve its terminal outcome and exclude it from subsequent automatic drains (daily-ai-news::CON-001/AC-01).
- Incoming events queue while delivery is running: select and reserve the next report inside the serial operation, using current persisted status (daily-ai-news::CON-001/AC-03).
- A new day arrives while older reports await recovery: generation may complete, but its content stays queued behind them. First reconcile any newer usable conversation; only an actual remaining prerequisite blocks the queue (daily-ai-news::REQ-002/AC-02, daily-ai-news::REQ-001/AC-03).
- An unchanged snapshot, repeated label update or restart after a stale-context rejection does not reuse the same failed context. Context refresh during an in-flight attempt remains newer than the captured attempt context and may unlock a subsequent attempt. Duplicate snapshot/inbound paths share the serial drain and never duplicate accepted or uncertain submissions.
- Existing credential/context status corrections use the existing notice surfaces and qualify for the pure factual wording exception. The source-degradation state uses the reviewed seven-state message reference. Missing context, stale credentials, account/target mismatch, disable and close continue to block submission.
- Disable, target change, or account change during an in-flight submission: settle that attempt and stop before the next (daily-ai-news::CON-001/AC-02).
- Closing the scheduler stops the drain before its next submission. The main Weixin turn interprets the retry/refresh request and inspects the common owner. Trusted inbound updates channel context independently; a research retry applies only to a same-day no-output failure (including imported legacy archive-stage failures), while existing output follows cached recovery. Busy requests return a refusal.
- No saved message or no eligible report: no recovery submission. Failed research does not invent content. The current same-day retry policy remains the baseline; the resilience closure rules below distinguish a finalised degraded report from a no-output failure.
- After cutover, the [shared scheduled store](scheduled-tasks.md) permanently retains execution summaries and protects pending/paused bodies from settled-detail cleanup under scheduled-tasks::REQ-014, and preserves same-day retryable automatic failures until settlement/deletion or expiry. Import includes every surviving legacy record; formerly pruned content cannot be recovered or regenerated as history.
- Operational failure notifications remain separate from report content; the confirmed missing-source statement belongs inside the report under daily-ai-news::REQ-006/AC-02. Manual preview/send remains an explicit user operation rather than part of the automatic backlog.
- News remains bounded plain channel text under [scheduled-tasks::CON-001/AC-04](scheduled-tasks.md). Fetched instructions, markup, links or media grant no management, execution, local-resource or extra delivery authority. No new renderer or automatic media loading is proposed; source-degradation presentation follows the approved message reference.
- Summary generation keeps the article's claim, negation, attribution and material limits together (daily-ai-news::REQ-004/AC-01). String validation checks length, sentence endings and ellipsis markers; it cannot prove semantic accuracy.
- New short summaries reserve enough space for ten articles, title/source metadata and section headings. Detailed summaries remain in the archive. Empty, punctuation-only, overlong, ellipsized or unfinished short text enters the bounded rewrite path (daily-ai-news::REQ-004/AC-02, daily-ai-news::REQ-004/AC-03).
- Previously frozen messages are not regenerated during recovery. Old long previews receive an explicit legacy-summary notice; old stored digests remain readable (daily-ai-news::REQ-004/AC-04).

### Resilience operation sequences

These operation sequences implement the accepted requirements and their reviewed boundaries.

| Sequence | Expected boundary |
|---|---|
| HN fails after bounded attempts; AI content completes | Keep the valid content and identify missing HN: daily-ai-news::REQ-006/AC-01 and daily-ai-news::REQ-006/AC-02. An archive failure does not block delivery under daily-ai-news::REQ-007/AC-01. |
| HN succeeds; AI fails, or one source yields some usable articles before failing | retain each committed article, mark the affected source degraded, identify its shortfall and settle a degraded report. Do not pretend a failed candidate was successfully excluded or discard the successful source. |
| A fresh HN execution encounters only previously delivered articles | Reuse successful summaries and select the current eligible hot-list items under daily-ai-news::REQ-008/AC-02; this is a new occurrence, not replay of a settled report. General-news delivery deduplication remains in force. |
| Both sources fail and no usable news exists | no report; preserve article checkpoints and use the existing bounded same-day research retry eligibility. No new automatic retry of manual or execution-unknown occurrences. |
| Transient request error, persistent error, source deadline, overall deadline or cancellation | retry only eligible reads within the remaining budget; preserve completed work; stop or fence the worker before releasing its reservation. Cancellation never grants delivery. |
| A/B/C have summaries, D fails; next eligible attempt encounters all four | Reuse A/B/C and process unfinished D only: daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-02 and daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-03. A new day/ranking is not summary invalidation. |
| Two candidates/occurrences encounter the same article simultaneously | One effective processing claim; others reuse its committed result or report known-but-unavailable while processing is unresolved. Unknown ownership is not stolen on a timer: daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-04. |
| Body saved, then crash before summary; model returns, then crash before commit; summary committed, then archive fails | distinguish body-ready, uncertain processing and successful summary. Reuse only durable evidence; do not automatically repeat an uncertain model operation. A committed success survives downstream failure: daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-01 and daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-03. |
| Archive fails after successful article processing | Keep results; do not refetch or regenerate. Eligible delivery proceeds, while archive work retains its own retry outcome: daily-ai-news::REQ-007. |
| A degraded body is frozen; old request returns or an old retry wake fires | one atomic finalisation closes research retries and rejects late body replacement, even while channel prerequisites are missing. |
| Context recovers after report freeze; channel accepts all, some or an uncertain number of parts | Send exact saved content in eligible order; settle the actual channel outcome independently of content completeness: daily-ai-news::REQ-001, daily-ai-news::REQ-002 and daily-ai-news::CON-001. |
| Task paused, deleted or edited while research/archive is running | Existing revision/pause/ownership rules apply; captured attempts may settle but no stale worker starts an unauthorised effect. archive-only retries use the same owner and explicit eligibility, not a separate unguarded background loop. |
| Successful summary asset missing/corrupt; storage quota reached; settled execution details are cleaned | Preserve success/dedup evidence and pending references; no false cache success or automatic summary regeneration: daily-ai-news::REQ-005/AC-05. Admission bounds and conservative legacy handling are defined below. |
| New run uses the same date/instruction as an incomplete older preview | new discovery receives its own source state; article results can be reused, but the old preview is not proof that new research ran. A frozen delivery recovery keeps its original source outcome. |
| Trace start/end missing after a process exit | Persisted state is authoritative. Missing logs mean insufficient observation, not proof that nothing ran; existing unknown/no-replay rules apply. |
| User requests reanalysis, changes summary requirements or asks for another research task | Ordinary run-now still uses daily-ai-news::REQ-005. A new rewrite operation or cross-task compatibility policy is outside this revision; do not silently invalidate summaries. |

Content-admission sequences (daily-ai-news::REQ-009): a candidate yields an unrelated ad → reject before success and try the next rank; only a title/navigation/login text is available → insufficient, no fabricated news; model verdict/evidence is missing, malformed or not in the supplied body → fail closed; translated/paraphrased titles with supporting body → accept; the one brief rewrite changes its verdict → reject the rewritten result; content rejection followed by a later eligible run → read fresh body, without automatically retrying the rejected candidate in the same run; success followed by archive failure or a later ranking → unchanged saved summary; deadline/cancellation before commit → existing fencing and unknown rules, never a late success. Candidate metadata and page text remain untrusted data, including instructions embedded in either. A later encounter with legacy success triggers one validation only; match → reuse unchanged; mismatch/insufficient → exclude new selection but retain audit; concurrent encounter → unavailable until settlement; malformed output, model failure, cancellation or owner loss → no repeated validation or late admission.

## Implementation Decisions

Use the common Scheduled Task ownership and recipient dispatcher after [ADR-0016](../adr/0016-daily-news-owner-handoff.md) handoff. Default timing is 08:30 Asia/Shanghai; saved task rules are editable and use common latest-only downtime and intentional-pause semantics. No-output automatic research failures (including imported legacy archive-stage failures) retry after 10, 30, then 60 minutes within the same Shanghai day; explicit retry preserves an existing future retry time, and pause/deletion cancels it. The source JSON becomes a fence and prepared snapshot; the committed common store owns future work. Recovery reconciles persisted channel refresh evidence with each saved report on startup, channel updates and scheduled work, as well as inbound dispatch. All paths use the same serial date-ordered drain; an event alone is not durable recovery state. Keep cache storage and generation independent from delivery recovery. Preserve ready records with content on restart. Resolve bundled process paths through a shared runtime resolver instead of relying on an obsolete startup string.

Generate a separate short prose summary alongside the detailed summary from the same article. Reserve a conservative per-article text budget that fits the existing ten-article layout. Validate before accepting research output, and rewrite invalid short output once with the original source context. Formatting preserves valid short text verbatim instead of compressing four fields. The report uses the approved plain-text message presentation; there is no new client dashboard.

### Resilience implementation design

The common owner persists article results, source settlement and archive jobs separately, following ADR-0017. Numerical bounds below are engineering defaults verified at controlled boundaries; they are not a live-network latency guarantee.

**Content admission.** The existing per-article summary response carries a matched/mismatched/insufficient assessment. The host checks a bounded supporting excerpt against the exact supplied body (up to the existing input limit), as well as the existing URL, section, time and summary checks. This validates evidence provenance, not semantic truth by substring matching; topic correspondence remains the model's explicit task. Save content-rejection state so a later eligible attempt does not repeatedly summarize a known wrong body. Successful body/summary bytes and frozen delivery remain immutable. For legacy successes without assessment, reserve and persist a one-time validation claim, then store a separate eligibility assessment. An interrupted/failed validation is unavailable and never automatically retried; this excludes the article from new selection without erasing its historical success or audit.

**Article results and archives.** Use the existing canonical article URL identity, scoped to the current trusted daily-news owner. Normalise known tracking differences, not titles or arbitrary query parameters. Retain canonical/source references, the successfully fetched body or its immutable reference, detailed summary, short summary and validation/provenance. HN item identity, rank, score and comments are discovery metadata; they do not replace article identity or invalidate its summary. A content checksum verifies saved-asset integrity and binding, not daily freshness. Cross-URL aliases and semantically different pages are not automatically merged.

Processing persists running, failed, unknown or succeeded. A running record may contain a saved body; a model response is provisional until commit; only a durable validated result plus success commit is reusable. Retryable failure may be claimed again only under an eligible execution; unknown requires reconciliation. A successful result is immutable for ordinary encounters. A single SQLite transaction commits result JSON, checksum and success together in the scheduled owner database. There is no separately published file reference to mistake for a committed result. A worker whose processing authority has been fenced cannot publish late article success or overwrite a committed result.

Archive means the user-readable local Markdown/Obsidian article body and source metadata, the daily detailed-summary note, and HN ranking/summary notes. It is distinct from the reliable article-result store and from the final Cached Output. Archive rendering reads saved bodies and summaries, so neither network failure nor an already existing archive path can silently substitute or delete a successful result. Legacy records are imported only with evidence: an old success key without recoverable summary bytes remains known-but-unavailable, not a newly unseen article and not a valid cache hit. This revision does not automatically reconstruct old missing article assets from historical notes. A retained key without a new valid result remains unavailable; no silent mass refetch or rewrite occurs.

**Source settlement and retry budget.** For scheduled occurrences, persist source start and complete/empty/degraded/failed outcomes; an interrupted occurrence remains execution-unknown. Preview keeps its own durable work claim and final digest. A successful empty discovery has an empty outcome with zero eligible articles and an explicit shortage against its requested count; a request exception is failed, or degraded if usable articles survived. Commit useful results as they arrive rather than only after the combined researcher returns. Initially preserve the HN-then-AI order because AI selection uses HN identities, but reserve time for AI and release HN work at its own deadline. HN failure cannot prevent AI from starting; AI failure cannot erase committed HN results.

Eligible public GET reads make at most three attempts: 15 seconds per request, 250/750 ms backoff plus up to 99 ms jitter, and Retry-After capped at 2 seconds. Retry ECONNRESET, ETIMEDOUT, EAI_AGAIN, ENETUNREACH, ECONNREFUSED, request timeout and HTTP 408/429/500/502/503/504. Cancellation, permanent HTTP failures, invalid input, model calls and storage writes do not use this retry helper. Before discovery, an isolated structured model call resolves the frozen instruction into source counts, with a 30-second deadline and host validation of integers, supported fields and the ten-article total. It has no management or recipient authority; invalid output fails instead of silently reverting to defaults. Without an explicit source/count change the default remains five plus five. A zero count skips that source. Each selected source has a six-minute envelope including all requests and model work; sequential HN then AI therefore has at most twelve minutes of source work plus parameter resolution. Abort/fence late results before admitting later work. The existing one-time short-summary rewrite remains separate. These bounds were exercised with controlled I/O, not a live-network performance benchmark.

Archive attempts have a 30-second deadline; after an initial failure they retry after 10, 30 and 60 minutes, at most four attempts, independently of the report date and delivery outcome. Paused/deleted tasks cannot start another automatic archive effect. Each attempt checks current ownership and its attempt number; late completion cannot replace a newer outcome. Preview archive jobs retain their own snapshot rather than being overwritten by a newer preview. New archives add a stable, generation-ordered subdirectory inside each date; this prevents an older retry from overwriting a newer same-day report and preserves newest-first lookup. Existing legacy paths are not moved. Retry repairs a partial existing snapshot from saved content using compare-and-swap.

**Report finalisation and delivery.** Aggregate durable source outcomes into complete/degraded/failed content independently of archive and channel outcomes. Reserve room for the missing-source statement within the existing whole-message limit; never remove a material qualification or mechanically truncate a summary to fit it. Symmetric degradation handles either source failing; the confirmed HN branch remains daily-ai-news::REQ-006. When a valid body and the required article results are durably available, one transaction freezes exact text, source outcomes and result references and closes research retries for that occurrence. Execution can succeed at producing a deliverable while its content is degraded. Existing channel `partial` continues to mean partially accepted parts, never missing news sources.

A late request, old timer or later source recovery cannot rewrite this frozen body or automatically push a supplemental report. No-output research failures retain the current same-day 10/30/60-minute eligibility; finalised complete/degraded reports do not enter that loop. Archive-only work reads saved results and cannot create another research or delivery attempt. A later independently authorised execution may discover new candidates while consulting the retained article results and delivery history. Pauses, deletion, unknown execution and all terminal delivery protections remain in force.

**Retention and observability.** Article records reserve 2 MiB + 32 KiB before processing; total live/reserved article bytes are capped at 512 MiB and identities at 100,000. Completed records release unused reservation bytes. General discovery admits at most 20 candidates and HN at most 10. Validated source counts select at most ten articles in total; the source deadline and article admission limits also bound per-occurrence work. No successful result/identity is evicted or expired to reopen processing. A capacity/storage error prevents new generation with a storage-repair action while existing frozen output stays intact. Pending/running archive occurrences are protected from the shared settled-detail cleanup under scheduled-tasks::REQ-014. Preview archiving admits up to 200 pending jobs and retains the latest 31 terminal jobs; overflow is a visible archive failure, not a report-generation failure. No automatic cleanup of successful article assets is provided in this revision.

Persist scheduled source start/outcome and error classification before deriving status. New source outcomes and tracing include requested/selected counts, and a time-eligible candidate shortage is distinguished from a failed read. HN selection checks current submission metadata; summary reuse does not replace current rank/score metadata. Final general-news counts account for its retained delivery deduplication. Correlate tracing with occurrence, source, article and attempt, including cache hit, request retry, summary persistence, source settlement, report freeze, archive and delivery. Durable claim evidence distinguishes not claimed from claimed with uncertain execution; neither a missing span nor elapsed time proves not triggered. Tracing never controls retries or state transitions, exposes credentials, or changes external-send authority. Operational notifications retain existing coalescing; historical-notice replay remediation remains separate.

- The archive gate and article reuse are explicitly approved; mandatory result/message storage remains a hard prerequisite.
- The user continued after the concrete message prototype. Its static syntax and seven states were checked; automated rendering of that local file was blocked by the browser URL policy and is not claimed. Actual message text/state is exercised through fixture ingress.
- The earlier resilience increment was deployed as `20261016-news-0968ee7` at the user’s explicit request; [dated deployment evidence](../.workings/daily-ai-news/test-report.md#deployment-verification). The later current-selection/history release has its own acceptance record. The REQ-009 repair has final gates and six authorized short actual-model samples passed; [current report](../.workings/scheduled-tasks/content-consistency/test-report.md). It was deployed as `20261018-content-consistency-7468a15` on 2026-10-18 with source/build/state checks passed; the user confirmed real Weixin acceptance of the title/body repair on 2026-10-20.
