# Scheduled tasks

> Related decisions: [ADR-0015](../adr/0015-durable-scheduled-reminders.md), [ADR-0016](../adr/0016-daily-news-owner-handoff.md)

## Overview

Users can create and maintain one-time, fixed-interval and daily/weekly calendar reminders or public-web research tasks in bound private chats and authorized Feishu groups. Existing AI daily news remains managed through personal chats. The service saves the settings and complete text, then executes independently of the creating conversation turn.

## Capabilities

- Create a one-time, fixed-interval or daily/weekly calendar reminder or research task in natural language. Research runs at execution time and produces complete prose with numbered source links. The receipt shows its task number, saved reminder or research requirement, resolved rule, next time, timezone, enabled state and current-conversation destination. Shanghai time is the default; missing, contradictory or invalid settings require clarification or correction.
- Create or explicitly edit ordinary reminders as complete messages addressed to you, excluding scheduling instructions. “每分钟提醒我活动一下” becomes “你应该活动一下了”. Explicit original-text reminders preserve the supplied wording, spaces and line breaks without an added title; the receipt previews the saved message.
- List current tasks, completed tasks or all saved tasks. Lists default to the current notification conversation; explicitly asking for all tasks in a private chat includes both bound private channels. In a group, all lists, counts, history and direct references remain limited to that group; authorized members share management. Completed and deleted tasks leave the default current list; an empty current list still distinguishes saved completed history.
- Inspect settings and execution/delivery history by task number or name, including deleted tasks and, in personal chats, the existing daily news; duplicate names require a choice. Daily news retains its specialised summaries and archive, without creating a replacement task.
- Change a task's name, reminder content or research requirement, and future rule together when requested. A combined request can change selected settings and immediately run with those changes. Name/content edits preserve the original timing; changing the rule starts a new future cadence. Editing leaves a paused task paused and preserves already-started executions and text; conflicting edits require a fresh read.
- Disable future automatic execution and cached delivery. Every new explicit disable also revokes earlier manual delivery authority. Enable future execution and eligible caches. Recurring tasks resume at their next future slot without backfilling paused time; a one-time deadline missed while paused needs a new time or an explicit run-now.
- Explicitly execute once using the current saved content, scope, schedule and enabled state, without restoring older settings from conversation or memory. On a paused task, only that new execution is authorized to send until another disable. Busy or capacity-limited requests explain why they did not start.
- Delete an idle task to stop future execution and cancel unsent cached results while retaining its task number and history. An operation in flight must settle first; disable can stop further submissions while waiting.
- Distinguish generation, pending delivery, unsuccessful or uncertain outcomes and completed tasks. One-time completion needs successful generation and complete channel acceptance; recurring tasks remain current for later runs; no next time alone is insufficient. An unresolved older execution is not hidden by later success.
- Read retained instructions, reminder text and output in pages. Execution summaries have no count limit and remain queryable newest first or by execution identity, including after task deletion. Cumulative execution counts exclude skipped time ranges; tasks with possibly missing older history report a known minimum. Settled output and detailed records expire by configured age or capacity, with cleanup clearly disclosed. Pending/paused output, in-flight work, unfinished archives and pending failure notices remain protected; article summaries and local archives remain available.
- Keep tasks, pause state, original interval timing and cached text across restarts. After downtime, execute only the latest missed slot and show older slots as a compact skipped range. Repeated management requests return their original operation and current task state without applying it again.
- Recover exact cached text when the original account and conversation become usable, oldest eligible planned time first. A manual result uses the time its request was accepted. Paused tasks do not block other eligible tasks.
- See current execution/delivery blockers and recovery actions; resolved channel conditions do not leave stale waiting advice. Failure notices are bounded and unchanged notices are not repeatedly sent; uncertain notices are not automatically resent.
- Prevent the same scheduled intent from becoming a second memory reminder, including after edit, disable or deletion. Unrelated plans in the same message remain available. Recognized task settings leave active memory in the same personal or group scope; independent preferences in the same message or saved note remain.
- At capacity, retain existing text and block new generation, including manual requests. Eligible caches can still drain. Supported text is sent completely in channel-sized parts without ellipsis truncation.

## Boundaries and non-goals

- Tasks support a future instant or one-time relative delay (such as “两分钟后”), positive fixed intervals of at least one minute, and a local daily time on all or selected weekdays. A relative delay resolves once when accepted and is not moved by replay or restart. Only an explicit repeating request (such as “每两分钟”) creates recurrence. A fixed interval first runs one interval after creation or rule editing unless a future start is specified. Calendar times skip nonexistent daylight-saving times and use only the earlier repeated time. A task retains its reminder or research type; create a new task to switch type. A claimed one-time plan cannot be restarted by editing it.
- A reminder message stays fixed between content edits; changing only the name, timing or enabled state does not rewrite it. Older reminders keep their prior text until you explicitly edit the content. Already-started executions and saved messages retain their original text. Ambiguous content needs clarification; these reminders do not calculate changing countdowns.
- Deletion retains history but cannot be undone by enabling the same task. Create a new task when needed. History cleanup never turns an old failed or uncertain execution into a completed one.
- Each generated result stays with the account and conversation selected for that execution. Changing a destination does not redirect old cached results or resend historical outcomes. Weixin rebinding requires a fresh message under the new binding; rebinding alone is insufficient.
- Accepted, partially accepted, nonrecoverably rejected and uncertain submissions are never automatically replayed. A part already submitted cannot be recalled; channel acceptance does not confirm phone receipt. A later explicit run-now is a separate execution.
- Tasks cannot execute programs or files, download media, create further tasks or grant permissions. Markup and media links remain text. Current target permissions and pause state are checked before every new part.
- Research uses public webpages and requires actual search and source-page access. Generic research has a five-minute limit; a failure explains its stage and cause without inventing a report. Failed generic research and uncertain execution are not automatically repeated. Daily news retains its own bounded research/archive retry rules and content limits described in [Daily AI news](daily-ai-news.md). Oversized output fails without an abbreviated substitute. The service must be running for timely execution; unavailable service or channel can delay delivery.
